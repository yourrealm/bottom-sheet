Spike 01: single scroller

Things to try:

  1. From the medium detent, drag up in one motion: the sheet should expand, then the content should scroll without lifting your finger.
  2. Scroll deep into the content, then drag down in one motion: the content should reach its top, then the sheet should follow your finger.
  3. Scroll deep into the content, then fling down and let go: the content should stop at its top (guard), not close the sheet.
  4. From the medium detent, fling up: the sheet should stop at full and not shoot into the content.
  5. Fling down from medium: the sheet should close. Tap the dimmed area: it should close.
  6. Horizontal row: it should pan sideways without moving the sheet.
  7. Focus the input: watch the keyboard behaviour.
